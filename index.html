OTJ / DDT learning guide ↗KSBs & assessment ↗

One programme. Different perspectives.

Take the learning into your work.

Pick one resource and a live brief. These starting points are useful across roles, so explore more than one perspective.

01 / MAKE

Creating content

Turn an audience need into a clear piece of content.

  • Draft, design, record or edit a first version.
  • Explain the choices you made for the audience and channel.
  • Use feedback or data to improve it.
02 / SHAPE

Editing content

Strengthen the idea, the voice and the reader’s experience.

  • Write a brief with a clear angle and audience.
  • Review structure, accuracy, tone and accessibility.
  • Show how your changes improved the piece.
03 / ENABLE

Managing content

Connect the creative work to priorities, people and results.

  • Define the outcome, responsibilities and review points.
  • Make a practical contribution to developing the content.
  • Evaluate performance and propose the next improvement.
Make your contribution visible.

Record what you learned, what you personally did, why you made those choices and what changed. Use your programme brief and agreed assessment requirements to guide your evidence.

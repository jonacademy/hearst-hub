# Images and licence examples

The former stock-photo selection bank has been removed from Part 3. No Pexels, Geograph or Flickr photo data is included in this version of index.html or redistributed as standalone files in the ZIP.

Sections 1–2 retain the existing illustrated workplace stand-in and an optional learner-owned photo, kept locally in browser memory. The learner is responsible for selecting an appropriate workplace photo.

Part 3 uses text-based licence badges and fictional source records, not AI-generated or stock photographs. “Desk light” by Sam Patel and https://example.org/training/desk-light are explicitly illustrative attribution data, not a real licensed photograph or a source to download.

Licence examples describe CC BY 4.0, CC BY-SA 4.0, CC BY-ND 4.0, CC BY-NC 4.0 and CC0 1.0. No official CC logo artwork is reproduced. Links to Creative Commons and UK IPO guidance appear in the activity and PDF. Source review: 4 November 2026.
